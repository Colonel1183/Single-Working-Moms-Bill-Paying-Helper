Ask yourself:
“What are the necessities that have to be paid so I can live, work, and care for my loving family?”

These are the things that keep you safe and your home running smoothly: (Make your own list)

Rent or Mortgage Food Transportation
Transportation Insurance Childcare (if needed)

Next ask:
“What bills can wait a week or two, or be negotiated?”

These bills are usually more flexible:

Phone Internet Credit cards
Cable Small debts Subscriptions

(Make your own list)

Make a simple list of your lenders and service providers. Start with the one that feels the most urgent or the most stressful.

When you call, be:

  • Polite
  • Calm
  • Honest
1. Make your first important payment as soon as you can.
Even a partial payment shows good faith.
2. Make your first phone call today.
Write a short script so you feel prepared and steady.
You can say something like this:
“I’m going through a financial hardship right now. Can you work out a temporary hardship plan with me? I want to stay in touch and honor whatever agreement we make.”
Just making one phone call will help to reduce the pressure you have been feeling.

Take a breath (smile).

You have a plan.
You took action.
You moved forward.
Keep working your plan.
